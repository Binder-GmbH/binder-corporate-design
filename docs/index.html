The triad

Black, white and BINDER Red produce the primary BINDER colour impression. This triad is the core of the corporate design and a primary design element in its own right. Everything else supports it and must never dilute its contrast.

Gray scale 100 lightest · 900 darkest · white and black sit outside the ramp

The scale runs 100 (lightest) to 900 (darkest), the same direction as CSS font-weight. It is denser at the light end on purpose: pale surface tones get used over large areas, where small differences matter.

Typography

The corporate typeface is Source Sans 3, designed by Paul D. Hunt. It is open source and free to use — no licence has to be purchased. Get it from Google Fonts, Adobe Fonts or GitHub. This page is set in it.

Weights in use: Light 300, Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600.

Source Serif 4 for quotations and emotional text

The serif companion. Same origin as Source Sans 3, also open source and free: Google Fonts, Adobe Fonts, GitHub.

Rules

  • Primary text colour is 100 % black. BINDER Red for emphasis, grays to de-emphasise. White on red or dark backgrounds.
  • Minimum size 6 pt — including text inside charts and diagrams.
  • Kerning: optical.
  • Old-style figures in body copy, lining figures in headlines, tabular figures in tables.

The red line above titles

A short horizontal red line sits directly above main titles. All three measurements derive from the font size, so the rule holds at any scale.

PropertyRule
ColourBINDER Red
PlacementDirectly above the title, always flush left
Thickness0.10 × font size
LengthWidth of the first three letters of the title
Distance to title0.3 × font size

At 50 pt type: thickness 5 pt, distance 16.6 pt.

Clear space and position

The measure is X, the height of the Bildmarke. Keep 1.5 × X clear on all sides. Place the logo top right, 2 × X from the format edge. The positioning distance is not the clear space; observe both.

Minimum sizes

VersionMinimum
Logo with claim30 mm

Below 30 mm width, drop the claim — it is no longer legible.

Never

  • Use the word mark without the Bildmarke
  • Replace the typeface
  • Crop, compress, distort or rotate it
  • Add further elements to it
  • Recolour it beyond black, white or full colour
  • Use the Bildmarke as a bullet, watermark or decoration
