# Typography

## Corporate typeface: FF Meta Pro

Designed by Erik Spiekermann. A humanist sans-serif that carries the BINDER
appearance across print and screen.

**FF Meta Pro is a licensed typeface.** Every weight requires a purchased
licence. It is not bundled in this repository. Sources: [linotype.com](https://www.linotype.com),
[fontshop.com](https://www.fontshop.com).

If you are working on BINDER material and need the fonts, ask the marketing
department before buying a licence yourself — there may be an existing
agreement that covers you.

### The family

| Family | Use |
|---|---|
| **Meta Pro** | All standard applications, every weight |
| **Meta Headline OT** | Optional for very large type — trade-show stands, posters |
| **Meta Serif Pro** | Emotional text: quotations, testimonials |

### Substitutes

Use these only where FF Meta Pro cannot be installed or licensed.

| Instead of | Use | When |
|---|---|---|
| Meta Pro | **Arial Unicode MS** | Office applications, Word/PowerPoint templates, Chinese characters |
| Meta Serif Pro | **Georgia** | Quotations in substitute settings |

Meta Pro is deliberately **not** used in Office applications. The Word
templates are built on Arial so they render identically on every workstation.

## Type rules

- **Primary text colour is 100 % black.** BINDER Red for emphasis, grays to
  de-emphasise. On red or dark backgrounds, set type in white.
- **Minimum type size is 6 pt** — including text inside charts, diagrams and
  infographics. Always check legibility at final size.
- **Kerning: optical.**
- **Figures:** old-style figures in body copy, lining figures in headlines,
  tabular figures in tables.
- On coloured backgrounds always keep enough contrast for the text to stay
  readable.

## Hierarchy

Sizes below are for A4 reading matter. Scale all other formats
proportionally. ZAB = leading (Zeilenabstand).

| Level | Typeface | Size | Leading |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Meta Pro Bold | 22 pt | 27.5 pt |
| Subline | Meta Pro Light | 22 pt | 27.5 pt |
| H2 | Meta Pro Book | 15 pt | 16.5 pt |
| H3 | Meta Pro Bold, all caps | 10 pt | 11 pt |
| H4 | Meta Pro Book | 9 pt | 11 pt |
| Body copy | Meta Pro Book | 9 pt | 12 pt |
| Lists | Meta Pro Book | 9 pt | 12 pt |
| Captions | Meta Pro Light | 7 pt | 11 pt |
| Running head, page numbers | Meta Pro Bold with Meta Pro Light | 8 pt | — |
| Language marker on cover pages | Meta Pro Light, caps, white | 12 pt | — |

**Lists:** bullet point as the marker, 3.5 mm from the text. Wrapped lines
indent to align with the first line.

**Exception — product catalogue and price list.** Their density calls for a
smaller H1: Meta Pro Bold 18 pt / 22 pt leading, subline Meta Pro Light
18 pt / 22 pt. Headline and subline preferably sit on one line, separating
the series from the model designation. All other levels stay as above.

## Applying the hierarchy

Structure the content before you set it. Every topic needs exactly one clearly
most important headline (H1), optionally followed by a subline that goes into
detail. Headlines may run to several lines, but say it as briefly as possible.

Levels can be skipped where they are not needed — content of equal weight gets
headlines of equal weight. Within body copy and lists, individual words or
passages may be picked out with a different weight (bold, italic); use colour
for this very sparingly.

Captions are set only where they add something the image does not already say.

Typographic highlights and text inside callouts or flashes sit outside this
system and are treated as design elements.
